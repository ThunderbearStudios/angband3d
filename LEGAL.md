## 2. Strict Zero-Monetization Policy

To protect the community, contributors, and the integrity of this open-source fan work, **Angband 3D strictly prohibits any form of commercialization or monetization**:

1. **Free Access**: The game is 100% free to play, explore, download, and modify.
2. **No Paywalls or Subscriptions**: There are no paywalls, registration fees, paid tiers, or paid early access.
3. **No In-Game Purchases**: The game contains zero microtransactions, loot boxes, virtual currency, NFTs, or cosmetics for sale.
4. **No Advertising**: The client, web application, and documentation are completely free of third-party advertisements, affiliate links, sponsored banners, and commercial trackers.
5. **No Donations**: The maintainers do not accept financial donations, tips, Kickstarter pledges, or Patreon subscriptions for this project.

Anyone creating builds, mirrors, or forks of Angband 3D must strictly respect this non-commercial mandate.
